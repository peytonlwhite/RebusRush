# Rebus Rush Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 28, 2026**

Rebus Rush is an iPhone word-and-picture puzzle game provided by FooWibble LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play Rebus Rush, connect an Apple account, contact us, or use related features. This policy covers Rebus Rush; it does not describe the separate betting products offered on the website hosting this page.

## Information we collect and why

### Player identifiers and saved games

Rebus Rush uses Google Firebase Authentication to create a guest player identifier when you start playing. You can play without providing a name or email address or connecting an Apple account. A guest identifier is still an identifier: game records are associated with it rather than being fully anonymous.

We store that identifier and gameplay records in Firebase, including coins, solved puzzles, attempt counts, purchased and revealed hints, challenge selections, streaks, completion dates, timer results, and relevant game preferences. We use these records to operate the game, preserve progress, provide rewards and hints, and restore saved play. They are linked to your player account.

### Optional Sign in with Apple

You may connect Sign in with Apple to save your progress and restore it on another device. Apple provides authentication information and an app-specific account identifier, which Firebase associates with your player account. Our Apple sign-in request does not ask for your name or email address. We do not receive your Apple password.

Connecting a new Apple identity preserves the existing guest account's progress. If the Apple identity already has a saved account, the app asks for confirmation before restoring that account; the current guest progress is not merged into it.

### Support messages and puzzle reports

If you use Contact Us, we collect the name, email address, message, player identifier, and submission time you provide through that form. If you report a puzzle, we collect the puzzle identifier, issue category, relevant attempted answer, any details you enter, your player identifier, and submission time. We use this information to respond to requests, investigate problems, and improve puzzle accuracy. These submissions are stored in Firebase and are available to authorized support or review personnel.

### AI answer checking

Some answers are checked using Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic. When this happens, your submitted answer and the puzzle's answer, explanation, and hints are sent to Google so it can evaluate whether the meanings match. Exact matching answers may be checked on the device without an AI request. Google may also process technical request and security information when providing the service. Processing and retention by Google are governed by the applicable Firebase and Gemini service terms and privacy information linked below. Please use the answer field for puzzle answers rather than personal or sensitive information.

### Advertising and technical information

Rebus Rush uses Google's AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads, including rewarded ads. Google documents that this SDK may collect IP addresses (which can be used to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, ad and product interactions, and diagnostic information such as crash and performance data. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, service operation, and fraud prevention as described in its policies.

Where iOS asks for tracking permission, you can allow or decline it. The app requests non-personalized ads when tracking permission is not authorized. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing, including information needed to serve, measure, and secure ads. You can manage the app's tracking permission in your iPhone's Settings.

Firebase services also process technical information needed for authentication, security, hosting and service operation, such as IP addresses and service, installation, or device-related identifiers. The app uses Firebase App Check to help protect backend requests and Firebase Remote Config to retrieve app configuration.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, Rebus Rush schedules game reminders locally on your device. Notification preferences can be changed in the app and in iPhone Settings. These reminders do not require providing us with an email address or phone number.

## Retention, deletion and your choices

Saved game records remain in Firebase until removed. Deleting the app from your phone does not by itself delete cloud records. We retain support and report records as needed to address requests and maintain the game; account-linked records are included in the in-app account deletion process described below. Service providers may retain separate technical, security or backup records under their own retention practices, and records may be retained where required by law.

If you have connected Apple, open **Settings → Save progress / Account → Delete account and data**. The app asks you to confirm with Apple, revokes the app's Apple authorization, and deletes the Firebase account, saved game records, and associated support messages and puzzle reports. The game then starts a fresh guest session.

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information, including requests concerning guest progress. We may ask for information needed to locate the relevant records and verify that the request is yours. If a guest account cannot be identified, we may be unable to locate its records. Do not send your Apple password or authentication codes.

You can play without linking Apple, choose whether to submit support requests or reports, and manage tracking and notification permissions in iPhone Settings. Privacy rights may vary depending on where you live; contact us with a request or question about your information.
